Our test, object by object

Evidence, stated plainly: no real users. This is our own break test on 28 everyday objects from a list written and committed before any run. Every row links to the exact object the numbers came from: open it, drag it, press Let the robot try, then Teach it, and see for yourself.

19 of 28came back from Lux3D with parts that open the way the sentence said (95% interval 49.3% to 82.1%)
4 of 19opened by the robot that only sees the shape (a random pull: 3 of 19)
15 of 19opened after one demonstration (56.7% to 91.5%; without it, 4 of 19)

"Opens" means every moving part ends more than half open. The demonstrations in the test are scripted stand-ins for a person's drag; in the app, the demonstration is your own drag. Reruns: evidence/score.py and evidence/teach.py in the repository, with the rule in evidence/EVAL_PLAN.md.
